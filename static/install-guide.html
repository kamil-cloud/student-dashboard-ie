Student Dashboard

Your courses, deadlines, classes, grades and GPA in one place — filled in from your transcript and Blackboard. Everything stays in your browser.

1 · Install the extension (2 minutes)

Works on a computer in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers. Not in Safari or Firefox, and not on phones.

Download the extension (.zip)
  1. Open the downloaded Student-Dashboard.zip to unzip it (double-click on a Mac, right-click → Extract All on Windows). You get a folder named Student Dashboard: move it somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents — the browser loads it from there.
  2. Unzip the file you received. You get a folder named Student Dashboard (keep it somewhere permanent, e.g. in Documents — the browser loads it from there).
  3. Open chrome://extensions in the address bar (Edge: edge://extensions, Brave: brave://extensions).
  4. Turn on Developer mode (top right corner).
  5. Click Load unpacked and choose the Student Dashboard folder.
  6. The dashboard opens by itself. Pin it: click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Student Dashboard.

2 · Set it up (3 minutes)

  1. Transcript (IE): download your Expediente Académico PDF from the student portal and drop it in. It is read on your computer — never uploaded — and only courses and grades are kept.
  2. Blackboard: just be logged in to Blackboard in the same browser. The extension uses that session (it never asks for your password) and only reads: it never changes anything on Blackboard.
  3. Tick your courses and let it read the syllabi for grade weights: a small window opens for about a minute and closes by itself.

After that it syncs by itself every few hours while your browser is open, and reminds you before deadlines.

Good to know

  • Privacy: no account, no server. Your grades never leave your browser.
  • Backups: Settings → Backup & data → Export backup. The weekly backup file option saves one to Downloads automatically. Removing the extension deletes its data, so export first.
  • Phone: Settings → Connections → Export to calendar puts your deadlines and classes, with reminders, in your phone’s calendar.
  • Updating: replace the folder with the new version, then click the reload ↻ icon on the extension card in chrome://extensions. Your data is kept.
  • “Log in to Blackboard to sync”: your Blackboard session expired — open Blackboard, log in, then click Sync.
  • On Windows, Chrome may warn about “developer mode extensions” at startup: choose to keep it.
  • No computer at hand? The website works without the extension too (transcript, deadlines, grades, calendar export) — only the Blackboard sync needs it.
